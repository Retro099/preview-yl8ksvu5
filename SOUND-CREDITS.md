# Sound credits

Except the avatar tap sound (below), all sounds on this site are generated live in the browser with the Web Audio API (sine and triangle tones, filtered). No third party audio files are used, so no licences or attributions are needed.

Sounds play only after a tap, and only when Sound is on.

## Friend sounds (v6.7, 30 Oct 2026)

- Shiga friend: a soft music-box run (four sine notes, G5 B5 D6 G6, about 1.4 s, very low volume).
- Aichi friend: a soft acoustic-style pluck (four triangle notes, G3 B3 D4 G4, low-pass filtered, about 1.5 s, low volume).

Both are synthesised in the browser, play only on a tap, and stay silent when Sound is off.

## Avatar tap sound (v6.20, 6 Nov 2026)

- File: `public/sounds/googoogaagaa-tap.mp3` (served at `/preview-yl8ksvu5/sounds/googoogaagaa-tap.mp3`).
- Plays once when someone taps the GooGooGaaGaa blob avatar. Silent when Sound is set to off (same `sound` setting as the friends train).
- Computer-made Japanese neural voice (Microsoft Edge TTS, ja-JP-NanamiNeural via edge-tts), pitched up to sound playful and child-like. It is not a recording of a real child or of any real person. Approved by the owner (the "playful" sample).
- This is the only audio file on the site; all other sounds are still synthesised in the browser.
